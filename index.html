Guggulu
Botanical Name :

  • Commiphora mukul Hook

    • Family Name :

  • Burseraceae

    • Local Name :

  • Guggula

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi –Gugal

  • English –Indian bedellium

    • Synonyms :

  • Indian bdellium

  • Gum guggul


    • Habitat :

  • Guggulu plant commonly grows in dry and arid regions and it is cultivated extensively because of huge demand.Found in arid and rocky tracks of Rajasthan,deccan,Bellary & Mysore


    • External Morphology :

  • Leaves: 1 to 3 foliate,leaflets sub sessile,the terminal leaf let is large,2 cm ling and 2 cm wide,rhomboid or ovate shaped,serrate,toothed in upper part

  • Fruit:Drupe,when ripe becomes red; 6-8 mm in diameter,ovoid ,epicarp is 4-valved

  • Seeds:Small,Nuts,Ovoid,Splitting into two


    • Useful Parts :

  • Niryasa(Gum resin)


    • RasadiPanchaka :

  • Rasa –Tikta Katu Madhura Kasaya

  • Guna –Tiksna,Sara,Suksma,Snigdha,Laghu,visada

  • Vipaka - Katu

  • Virya - Usna


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • Anti-inflammatory

  • Antimicrobial

  • Analgesic


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Vatavyadhi :Guggulu taken with milk is very useful as a Rasayana in all kinds of Vatavyadhi

  • AmavataRegular usage of Guhhulu with cow’s urine is beneficial in Amavata


    • Dose

  • Niryasa : 0.5-3g


    • Formulation:

  • Goksuradi guggulu

  • kancanara guggulu

  • Kaisora guggulu
